05 — How this is calculated (so you can check it)

  • Baseline time comes from the client, not an industry average. Captured during the discovery sprint or a follow-up interview, with the source stated on every report.
  • Hourly rate comes from the client's own fully-loaded labor cost for the role being augmented — never a number Tioga picks.
  • Volume is a real count from the governance ledger every agent action already writes to (the same log used for the audit trail and NIST AI RMF evidence mapping) — not a separate estimate.
  • Hours saved = (baseline minutes − AI processing minutes) × volume ÷ 60. AI processing time is near-zero at the latencies Tioga's own demo benchmarks show (~1.7s average for invoice-style extraction) — see How We Built the Invoice Processing Demo.
