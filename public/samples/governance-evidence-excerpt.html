Note what this row shows: the action never reached the ERP validation layer at all, because policy blocked it first. That's the point — evidence is logged for what didn't execute, with the same fidelity as what did.

03 — What a real engagement's version adds

  • Append-only storage (object-lock or equivalent) so the trail survives a restart and can't be edited after the fact
  • Your actual delegation-of-authority matrix in place of illustrative ceilings
  • Mapping to whichever framework you're building evidence for — NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, or a specific regulator/auditor questionnaire
